      A LITTLE MORE ABOUT US

      Grab Grocery

      A student front-end project for comparing everyday grocery prices in one simple place.

      Grab Grocery brings a sample catalog and store comparisons together to help shoppers explore their options before visiting a retailer. Browse the catalog, see prices side by side, then follow a product link to the store you choose.

      Platforms included

      Compare sample listings from these grocery platforms:

      • Blinkit
      • Zepto
      • Swiggy Instamart
      • BigBasket
      • JioMart

      What you can explore

      Search products by name, browse categories, and sort the results by popularity, lowest price, product name, or potential saving. Each product card displays the listed store prices together, highlights the lowest sample price, and includes links to search for that product on a retailer site.

      The catalog spans everyday essentials such as dairy, pantry staples, fresh produce, bakery, beverages, snacks, frozen foods, home care, personal care, baby care, and meat and seafood.

      How to use it

      1. Search for an item or choose a category to narrow the catalog.
      2. Compare the displayed prices and check which store has the lowest sample listing.
      3. Select a store search link to look up the item on that retailer's site.

      Sample data, not live offers

      Product prices, delivery fees, delivery times, and availability are sample data for this project. They are not live retailer prices and may differ from current listings, local stock, or checkout totals.

      Project and technology

      Grab Grocery is a responsive front-end demonstration built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It includes a demo sign-in screen for presentation; it does not create accounts, authenticate users, or save passwords. The comparison page can be browsed without signing in.
